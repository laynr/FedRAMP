    FedRAMP in 90 seconds

    FedRAMP is the U.S. government's single security-approval program for cloud services — in law at 44 U.S.C. §§ 3607–3616, run out of GSA. Prove your security once; the law gives that approval a formal “presumption of adequacy,” so agencies reuse it instead of re-assessing from scratch.

    Three things the internet still gets wrong

    “You need an agency sponsor.” Not since the 2026 rules — FedRAMP reviews and certifies directly.
    “The JAB reviews your package.” The JAB no longer exists; a FedRAMP Board + GSA program office replaced it.
    “Budget 12–18 months for review.” GSA reports review timelines around five weeks now. (Your preparation time is still yours.)
