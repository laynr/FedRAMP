# Take-home assignment (paraphrased)

> The original assignment text is deliberately not republished here — it belongs to
> Anthropic and may be reused for future candidates. Below is a brief paraphrase of the
> constraints this project was scoped against.

- Build a deployed, self-contained functional prototype under one of three themes:
  exploration/understanding, creative/generative tools, or systems/reliability.
- Target ~1–2 hours of work, with a hard cap of 8 hours; scoping ability is part of
  what's evaluated.
- The prototype must be evaluatable without the reviewer supplying data or domain
  expertise (bundle sample data or a demo mode).
- Deliverables: a GitHub repo, a live hosted prototype, a design rationale in both
  video (~5 min) and written form (including approximate time spent), and the AI
  transcripts from development.
- AI-assisted development is expected; what's evaluated is the candidate's judgment in
  directing it.

This project answers Theme 1 (Exploration & Understanding).
